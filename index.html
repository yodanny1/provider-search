Carrier Search

Individual Medicare Advantage near 92648 (UCLA uses 90045). Public guest searches, no broker login. Pick how you want to search and every carrier opens in its own tab.

Only one tab opened? Your browser blocked the rest as pop-ups. Click the blocked pop-up icon at the right end of the address bar, choose Always allow pop-ups, then click the green button again. You only need to do this once.

Put the green button on your desktop

  1. Keep this file and the green icon file provider-search-links.ico (Windows) or provider-search-links.png (Mac) together in a folder that will stay put, like Documents.
  2. Drag this green button onto your desktop:
    PROVIDER SEARCH LINKS MEDICAL GROUP SEARCH
  3. Windows: right-click the new desktop shortcut > Properties > Change Icon > Browse, pick provider-search-links.ico, then OK. Right-click it again > Rename and type PROVIDER SEARCH LINKS.
  4. Mac: open provider-search-links.png, press Command-A then Command-C. Click the desktop shortcut, press Command-I, click the small icon at the top left of the Info window and press Command-V. Click the name to rename it.
  5. Double-click the green icon at the start of a call. Every carrier opens in its own tab.

Your checkboxes, plan year and title are remembered by your browser. Links come from the carrier research done 2026-10-04. If a carrier lands on the wrong screen, tell Claude in the project and the link will be fixed.
